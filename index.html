FAQ

Common Questions

What services do you offer? +

We offer exterior painting, interior painting, deck staining & restoration, and custom mural & design work — for both residential and commercial clients across Greater Grand Rapids.

Are you actually the ones doing the work? +

Yes — it's just us, the owners. Jackson and Evelyn do all the work themselves. No subcontractors, no crew of strangers. Most college painting companies put beginners on the job; with Go Green you get owner-operators with real experience on every project.

Do you offer free estimates? +

Absolutely — estimates are always free and come with zero obligation. Fill out the form above or call us directly at (616) 264-2119.

What areas do you serve? +

We serve Greater Grand Rapids, Michigan and surrounding communities. Not sure if you're in our range? Give us a call — we're happy to travel for the right project.

Can you paint murals or custom designs? +

Yes! Co-owner Evelyn is an industrial design student with a lifelong love of illustration and painting. She specializes in custom murals for children's rooms, dining rooms, accent walls, and commercial spaces. If you can dream it, she can paint it.

How much does interior painting cost in Grand Rapids? +

Interior painting in Grand Rapids typically runs about $700 to $1,700 per room and $4,700 to $8,500 for a whole-house repaint, depending on square footage, ceiling height, prep work, and trim. We provide a fixed-price free estimate based on your specific home and project — call (616) 264-2119 or use the request form.

How much does exterior house painting cost in Grand Rapids? +

Exterior house painting in the Grand Rapids area typically ranges from $3,000 to $7,000 for a one- or two-story home, depending on siding type and condition, prep work, and accessibility. We provide free, fixed-price estimates after seeing the property.

Are you insured? +

Yes. Go Green College Painters carries full liability insurance on every job, with no exceptions. Proof of insurance is available on request.

How long does a typical painting project take? +

Most single-room interior repaints take 1 to 2 days. Whole-home interior repaints typically take 3 to 7 days. Exterior repaints generally take 3 to 6 days depending on home size, prep work, and weather. We provide a specific timeline as part of every free estimate.
