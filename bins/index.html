Garbage Bin Cleaning

Eco-friendly, high-pressure cleaning that kills 99.9% of germs, eliminates odors, and keeps pests away — serving Greater Grand Rapids.

The Service

High-Pressure. Eco-Friendly. Spotless.

Your garbage bins are one of the dirtiest things on your property — harboring bacteria, odors, and pests that no ordinary rinse can fix. Our professional bin cleaning service uses high-pressure, eco-friendly cleaning that kills 99.9% of germs and bacteria, eliminates foul odors, and drives away pests. We come to you, clean on-site, and leave your bins looking and smelling fresh.

99.9% Germ-Free

High-pressure hot water eliminates bacteria, mold, and pathogens that cause illness and odors.

Eco-Friendly

Biodegradable, non-toxic cleaning solutions that are safe for your family, pets, and the environment.

Pest Deterrent

Removes food residue and odors that attract flies, maggots, raccoons, and other pests to your bins.
