Occasions We Paint Banners For

  • High school & college graduations — the name, year, school colors, mascot, or any message you want the grad to remember.
  • Milestone birthdays — 1st, 16th, 18th, 21st, 30th, 50th, and everything in between.
  • Sorority & fraternity events — rush, COB, philanthropy, formals, theme parties. Evelyn has painted for Delta Zeta and is ready to make yours.
  • School & club events — spirit days, fundraisers, team banners, welcome signs.
  • Retirement parties — something to mark the end of a career that isn't a generic card.
  • Baby showers, bridal showers & bachelorettes — custom illustrated banners that double as keepsakes.
  • Holiday & Friendsmas parties — seasonal banners that actually get pulled back out every year.

Pricing

Custom banners range from $150 to $300 depending on size and complexity. A banner with clean lettering and a simple border is at the lower end; a fully illustrated banner with a detailed mascot, multiple motifs, or tight custom artwork runs higher. Every quote is free — reach out with your idea and Evelyn will give you a straight number.

How It Works

  1. Reach out — tell us the occasion, name, colors, any imagery or theme you love, and your event date.
  2. Concept sketch — Evelyn drafts a layout and shares it for your approval. Revisions welcome.
  3. Paint — once you've signed off, she paints the final banner by hand on butcher paper. Vinyl is available as an upgrade for a more durable finish.
  4. Pickup or delivery — we coordinate ahead of your event date in the Grand Rapids area.

Most banners take 5–10 days from order to finished. Plan ahead for graduation season — May and June book up fast.

What Makes These Different

Party store banners are generic and disposable. A custom painted banner is made for one specific person, hand-lettered and illustrated on butcher paper — sturdy enough for the party and worth keeping long after. Want something more durable? Vinyl is available as an upgrade. Either way: no clipart, no templates, no printing. Just paint and a real artist.

Common Questions

How much does a custom painted banner cost? +

Custom banners range from $150 to $300 depending on size and complexity. Simple text-focused banners are at the lower end; highly illustrated banners with detailed artwork are at the higher end. Every quote is free.

What occasions are custom painted banners made for? +

Graduations, milestone birthdays, retirement parties, baby showers, bridal showers, sorority and fraternity events, school events, holiday parties, and any other celebration worth remembering.

How long does it take to make a custom painted banner? +

Most banners take 5–10 days from order to delivery, including the design and approval step. Reach out early for graduation season — May and June book up fast.

Are the banners reusable? +

Yes — banners are painted on butcher paper, which holds up well for display and keeps as a keepsake. Vinyl is available as an upgrade for a more durable, weather-resistant finish.

Can I choose the design, colors, and text? +

Absolutely. Every banner is fully custom — you choose the name, message, colors, school colors, mascot, or any specific imagery or theme. Evelyn sketches a layout for your approval before any paint is applied.

Who makes the banners? +

Evelyn Befus designs and paints every banner by hand. She's an Industrial Design student at Wayne State University and a lifelong illustrator — no templates, no clipart, no printing.
