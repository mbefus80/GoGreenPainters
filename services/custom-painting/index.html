About Evelyn

Evelyn Befus is the Creative Director and co-owner of Go Green Painters. She's an Industrial Design student at Wayne State University and has been painting and illustrating since childhood. Every mural, every banner, and every custom design project is her work — no outsourcing, no AI generation, no shortcuts. Jackson handles project management and the painting crew; Evelyn handles everything that requires a real artist.

Custom painting work runs year-round and isn't weather-dependent, making it a natural complement to Go Green's seasonal exterior and interior work.
