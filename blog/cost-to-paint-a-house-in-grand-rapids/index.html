How Much Does It Cost to Paint a House in Grand Rapids?

By Jackson Befus · May 13, 2026

Most house painting projects in Grand Rapids fall between $700 and $9,500 depending on what you're painting. Interior repaints run roughly $700–$1,700 per room or $4,700–$8,500 for a whole house; exterior repaints run $3,000–$7,000 for a typical home; kitchen cabinet refinishing runs $2,500–$6,000; and deck staining runs $700–$2,500. Here's the full 2026 breakdown — what's included at each price point, and what pushes a quote higher or lower.

Interior Painting Costs in Grand Rapids

Interior painting is usually priced per room or as a whole-home package. As a 2026 guide for the Grand Rapids area:

  • Single room (bedroom, office): $700–$1,200
  • Living room or large room with vaulted ceiling: $1,000–$1,700
  • Whole-home interior repaint: $4,700–$8,500
  • Trim, doors, and baseboards (per room): $200–$500 on top of wall pricing
  • Ceilings: $150–$400 per room depending on height and condition

That pricing assumes two coats, standard 8–9 ft ceilings, minor wall prep (nail holes, small dings), and quality acrylic latex paint. Heavily damaged walls, dark-to-light color changes, and tall or detailed rooms push the number up.

Exterior Painting Costs in Grand Rapids

Exterior repaints in the Grand Rapids area generally run $3,000–$7,000 for a typical one- or two-story home. The biggest cost drivers are:

  • Home size and story count — more square footage and height means more labor and access equipment.
  • Siding type — vinyl and fiber-cement are quicker; cedar siding takes significantly more prep (tannin-blocking primer, knot priming) and runs at the higher end.
  • Current paint condition — peeling and failing paint needs scraping and spot-priming, which adds labor.
  • Detail elements — shutters, trim, dormers, railings, and multi-color schemes add time.

Larger executive homes — common in Cascade, Ada, and the Forest Hills area — frequently run $5,000–$9,000 because of size, cedar siding, and multi-story access.

Kitchen Cabinet Refinishing Costs

Refinishing kitchen cabinets is one of the highest-value painting projects you can do — a fraction of the cost of replacement, with results that look factory-applied when done correctly. In Grand Rapids, cabinet refinishing typically runs $2,500–$6,000 depending on:

  • Number of doors and drawer fronts
  • Whether you're painting the boxes as well as the doors
  • Current finish (raw wood, stained, or previously painted)
  • Color change complexity

The process matters more than the paint here: doors removed and labeled, surfaces degreased and scuff-sanded, a bonding primer, and two coats of cabinet-grade enamel. A typical kitchen takes 4–6 days.

Deck and Fence Staining Costs

Deck and fence staining in Grand Rapids generally runs $700–$2,500, depending on the size of the deck, the surface condition, and the type of stain. A standard 200–400 sq ft deck usually falls in the $900–$1,800 range. Transparent and semi-transparent stains cost less than solid stains, and a badly weathered deck needs more cleaning and sanding labor.

Note: this is refinishing and staining only. Board replacement and structural carpentry aren't part of a staining quote — those are a separate carpentry job.

What Drives a Painting Quote Up or Down

Two homes the same size can get very different quotes. The main factors:

  • Prep work needed — the single biggest variable. Sound surfaces paint fast; failing paint, water damage, or heavy cracking adds hours.
  • Surface material — cedar siding, stucco, and rough-sawn wood take more time and specialty primers than vinyl or smooth drywall.
  • Number of coats — dramatic color changes and deep colors often need an extra coat.
  • Height and access — second and third stories, steep lots, and complex rooflines require more equipment and time.
  • Detail and trim — lots of trim, shutters, railings, and multi-color schemes increase labor.
  • Paint quality — premium paints cost more per gallon but last years longer; we don't recommend cutting this corner.
  • Season — spring and fall are peak; booking in the shoulder season sometimes means more scheduling flexibility.

Does Your Neighborhood Affect the Price?

Indirectly, yes — not because painters charge more by ZIP code, but because home characteristics vary by area. Cascade, Ada, and Forest Hills have larger homes and far more cedar siding, so exterior quotes there tend to run higher. East Grand Rapids has older established homes with detailed trim and plaster walls, which adds prep time. The price tracks the house, not the address.
